LEADERSHIP MESSAGE

Leadership Messages

A message from Chairman and Headmaster

CHAIRMAN

Mr. Mohsin Ahmed

Building a strong foundation today for confident, responsible, and successful futures.

Education that builds knowledge, values, and a brighter future.

Dear Parents and Students,

At Mohsin Ahmed Urdu Primary School, we believe that education is the foundation upon which a child's future is built. Our aim is not only to provide quality education, but also to nurture responsible, confident, respectful, and compassionate individuals.

We strive to create a welcoming learning environment where every child is encouraged to develop curiosity, discipline, creativity, and a strong sense of responsibility. Along with academic learning, we value moral education, cultural activities, physical development, and the practical skills that help children grow with confidence.

We are committed to continuously improving learning opportunities for our students and providing them with the guidance and encouragement they need to discover their abilities and achieve their potential.

I sincerely thank our parents for their continued trust and support. Together, through the partnership of school, family, and community, let us guide our children towards a future filled with knowledge, character, confidence, and purpose.

Mr. Mohsin Ahmed
Chairman
HEADMASTER

Mr. Khan Mohammad Yasar

Guiding young minds with knowledge, values, and the confidence to grow.

Guiding every child towards learning, growth, and confidence.

Dear Parents and Students,

At Mohsin Ahmed Urdu Primary School, we believe that every child has unique abilities and deserves the opportunity to learn, explore, and grow. Our role as educators is to provide the right guidance, encouragement, and environment for every student to progress with confidence.

We give importance to strong academic foundations while encouraging students to develop good habits, moral values, discipline, curiosity, and respect for others. Special attention to subjects such as Mathematics and Science, along with cultural activities, physical education, computer education, and Islamic education with moral values, supports the all-round development of our students.

Our teachers work with dedication to understand the needs of each learner and make education meaningful and engaging. We also believe that the involvement and support of parents plays an important role in a child's educational journey

I encourage every student to learn with curiosity, participate with confidence, respect others, and always strive to become a better version of themselves. Together, we can create a strong foundation for their future.

Mr. Khan Mohammad Yasar
Headmaster
