ABOUT OUR SCHOOL


Mohsin Ahmed Urdu Primary School is a government-aided private minority ,urdu medium educational institution managed by Rani Laxmibai Mahila Kalyan Mandal , located in Aurangabad(Chh. Sambhajinagar),Maharashtra, committed to providing quality education and nurturing the overall development of every student. Established in 1999 by Mr. Mohsin Ahmed, the school has been dedicated to creating a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where students are encouraged to learn, explore, and develop their individual abilities. We believe that education goes beyond textbooks and examinations—it is about building knowledge, confidence, character, creativity, and responsible citizenship. Our school provides education for students from Class 1 to Class 7, following the Maharashtra State Board curriculum. Through dedicated teachers, appropriate learning resources, extracurricular activities, and a student-focused approach, we strive to make learning meaningful and engaging.

VISION


Our vision is not only to make the students aware of their syllabus but to make them sharper, brighter and be able to learn and understand curricular and co-curricular activities and also enabling them to fit in today's world.

MISSION


  • To provide quality and accessible education to every student.
  • To encourage curiosity, creativity, and independent thinking
  • To develop academic, social, cultural, and physical abilities.
  • To promote discipline, respect, values, and responsibility.
  • To create a safe and welcoming environment for students.
  • To prepare students to confidently face future opportunities and challenges

DEENIYAT EDUCATION

Along with academic learning, students are guided in Islamic education, moral values, discipline, respect, responsibility, and good character.

CULTURAL ACTIVITIES

Students are encouraged to participate in cultural celebrations and activities that help them appreciate their heritage, express their creativity, and build confidence.

SPECIAL ATTENTION TO MATHS & SCIENCE

We encourage students to develop strong foundations in Mathematics and Science through clear concepts, practical understanding, and engaging learning activities.

PLAYGROUND & PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT

Physical activities and playground experiences help students stay active, develop teamwork and coordination, and build healthy habits alongside their studies.

COMPUTER EDUCATION

Students are introduced to computers and digital learning to develop essential skills and prepare them to confidently use technology in their future education.

CURRICULAR & EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES

A balanced learning environment combines classroom education with activities beyond the curriculum, giving students opportunities to discover their interests, develop skills, and grow as individuals.
