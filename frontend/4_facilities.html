FACILITIES

Core Academic Facilities

Infrastructure that makes teaching more effective and learning more engaging.

CLASSROOM

Classrooms at Mohsin Ahmed Urdu Primary School provide a structured and welcoming environment for students to learn and participate in classroom activities. The classrooms are furnished with student desks and blackboards for regular teaching and instruction. Educational charts, subject-related displays, and student learning materials are arranged on the walls, creating a visually engaging learning environment.These spaces serve as an important setting where students develop their academic knowledge, participate in classroom activities, and build a strong foundation for their education.

COMPUTER LAB

The school's computer laboratory provides students with an opportunity to develop basic digital literacy and gain practical exposure to computers. The laboratory is equipped with desktop computers arranged systematically for student use, providing a dedicated space for learning essential computer skills. Through access to computers, students can become familiar with fundamental digital tools and develop confidence in using technology as part of their education. The computer lab also complements classroom learning by providing opportunities for students to explore digital resources and develop skills that are increasingly relevant to modern education.

ASSEMBLY HALL

The school's assembly area provides a shared space where students come together for collective activities, school gatherings, and important occasions. Such gatherings provide students with opportunities to participate in activities collectively, listen to important messages, and develop values such as cooperation, respect, responsibility, and unity. The assembly area therefore forms an important part of the school's daily and community-oriented activities.

SCHOOL GROUND

The school ground provides students with an open space for physical activity, exercise, recreation, and outdoor activities. Regular participation in such activities can encourage students to develop coordination, discipline, teamwork, and an active lifestyle. The ground therefore serves as a valuable space where students can engage in physical activities and enjoy learning and growing together outside the classroom.
